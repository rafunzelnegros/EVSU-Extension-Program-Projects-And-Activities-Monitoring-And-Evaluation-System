# Pre-Oral Final Adjustments

This build applies the last dashboard/workflow adjustments requested before the pre-oral defense:

- Solid EVSU maroon dashboard hero with white/gold accents (no hero gradient).
- Logged-in identity moved from beside Logout into the right side of the maroon hero.
- Personnel/Students visualization changed from a table to a vertical paired bar chart (Male/Female) with animation and per-bar hover details.
- PPA summary donut converted to an interactive SVG chart with hover details and smooth visual transitions.
- Active Partnerships now has visible maroon progress fills that respond dynamically to the counts.
- Board Confirmed dashboard now shows confirmed MOA and confirmed MOU rather than confirmed/unconfirmed.
- Partnership form has no Agreement Type field; Board Confirmed is a clear checkbox.
- PPA Data Entry is grouped into Basic Information, Approval/Board Confirmation, Training, Personnel, Impact Assessment, and Remarks.
- PPA approval date is disabled/cleared when Approved is not checked, and is required when Approved is checked.
- Director-created TAEP/QPAR reports become Approved immediately when the Director submits them; they do not enter the Reports review queue.
- Reports Approve/Return buttons have explicit readable styling.
- Replaceable EVSU logo placeholder added at `dashboard/static/dashboard/img/evsu-logo.png`.
- README includes logo replacement steps and UACS-code explanation.
